GROUPWORK

published [15/11/2026] ·

A group ins't formed for fun. A group is formed to achieve a common goal. My group was formed on the 25th of July 2026.

Before this group was formed, it was hard to interact because we barely knew each other. For this group to be formed, we first started doing revisions together and then later we had a permanent group which thrived through every path and we did not struggle.

Whenever there were disagreements or any challenging situations like debating over a complex question, we did not hesitate and we approched one the lectures who would break down the concept for us for easy undrestanding.

From the group experience I have learnt that once one is in a group, he/she should make sure that they interact and communicate for better understanding. This also helps one to boost confidence and one can give public speeches easily.
