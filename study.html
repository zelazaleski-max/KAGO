Study Tips & Resources

Strategies that have worked for me so far — feel free to try them.

🍅 Pomodoro Technique

Study in 25-minute focused blocks, then take a 5-minute break. After four rounds, rest for 15–30 minutes. It keeps my focus sharp and stops burnout.

🃏 Flashcards (Anki)

For definitions and key terms, Anki's spaced-repetition system helps me remember things long-term without last-minute cramming.

📅 Weekly Timetable

Every Sunday I plan my week — deadlines, study slots, and rest. Writing it down makes it real.

📊 Apply What I Learn

In Applied Business Computing, theory sticks when I practise it — rebuilding a spreadsheet, writing a small Python script, or sketching a business process diagram.
