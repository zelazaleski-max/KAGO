Study Tips & Resources

Strategies and systems that have worked for me so far.

🍅 The Pomodoro Technique

Study in 25-minute focused blocks, then take a 5-minute break. After four rounds, rest for 15–30 minutes. It prevents burnout and keeps my mind sharp during heavy coding or business theory sessions.

🃏 Spaced Repetition (Anki)

For definitions and key terms, I use Anki. Its spaced-repetition algorithm helps me remember facts long-term without last-minute cramming before exams.

📅 Weekly Time-Blocking

Every Sunday evening, I map out my week — classes, assignment deadlines, study slots, and rest. Writing it down makes it real and stops things from slipping through the cracks.

📊 Apply What I Learn

In Applied Business Computing, theory sticks when I practise it — rebuilding a spreadsheet, writing a small Python script, or sketching out a business process diagram.
