Reflecting on My Learning Journey

Published [15/11/2026] · 3 min read

When it's ones first rime in BAC, one may panic a lot because we are all unfamiliar with each other and may get lost in the campus while trying to find lecture rooms. On the first weeks I always thought the module of Systems Development was complicated and this made me feel like school is boring. I realised that there is a big difference between tetiary and high schools because at tetiary, one has to work hard unlike in high school.

The challenge

No one can say they never had to struggle first when they came to BAC. I had many challenges when BAC like balancing business modules with computing modules, adapt to a faster pace, learning to study independently and dealing with the postponement/delay of certain tasks.

What I changed

I adapted to BAC and adopted a few strategies like a weekly timetable, Pomodoro, study groups, library sessions, active recall, asking lecturers for help.

Growth

Right now I can work on a faster pace, independently, never postpone any tasks and I can balance my modules fairly. On week one, I used to be slow and found everything intimidating and I mostly relyed on other people. As for now, some people do not believe its me because of how do my things faster and independently.

Looking ahead

As for now, I always aim high and I make sure to do that precisely. For the next semester, there would be no limits to learning and I want to make sure that I always get higher marks. I want to make sure that there won't be any snicky things around.
